Services

We handle the whole process, from the first line of an idea to the final frame on screen.

Pre-production

The decisions made before the shoot are half the film.

  • Creative brief and concept
  • Script and voice-over copy
  • Storyboard and visual concept
  • Production plan

Production

We create the scenes no camera can reach with AI, and shoot the rest for real.

  • AI video and image generation
  • Live-action shoots and product photography
  • 3D animation and cartoons
  • Music videos
  • Voice-over

Post-production

In the edit, the story takes its final shape.

  • Editing and colour grading
  • VFX and motion graphics
  • Sound design and music
  • Vertical and social media formats

Strategy before video

Our co-founder’s ten years of product management experience brings a marketing mind to every project. Together we pin down what to say, to whom, and how.

Brand and launch strategy

A new product, a new venue or a new brand? We define the audience and the message, build the launch plan and produce everything from the launch film to the social media cuts.

Corporate training videos

Clear, memorable training content for sales teams, new hires and internal communications. We turn a complex topic into a short, focused video.

How we work

  1. We listen

    We talk about your business, your goal and the story you want to tell.

  2. We plan

    We prepare the strategy, script and storyboard for your approval.

  3. We produce

    We generate, shoot and edit the scenes, and design the sound.

  4. We deliver

    Your film arrives ready to publish, in every format you need.
